# Northstar source packet

All material in this packet is fictional and safe to use for practice.

## Sales note: prospective multi-site customer

**SLS-01:** A prospective customer operates eight locations.

**SLS-02:** The customer wants urgent faults handled differently from standard requests.

**SLS-03:** Sales wants to mention the capability in next week's proposal.

**SLS-04:** Operations must confirm what can be supported.

## Workshop extract: priority and urgent-work handling

**INT-01, sales director:** "The proposal should promise a response for critical faults, but I do not want to promise a dedicated team."

**INT-02, service manager:** "Priority 1 already means a complete loss of service at a customer site. The current contract says four business hours."

**INT-03, operations manager:** "During business hours, dispatch can see urgent work immediately. After hours, the duty supervisor monitors a separate queue."

**INT-04, regional manager:** "Supervisors change priorities when a technician finds that the fault is less serious than reported. We need an audit trail."

**INT-05, customer-data owner:** "Contract type is held in the customer record, but some older sites are missing the link to the parent account."

**INT-06, service director:** "Do not automate a different service promise until I have seen the staffing impact."

**INT-07, sales director:** "Could the system send an email to the account manager whenever a Priority 1 request is raised?"

## Sanitized customer-communication complaint

**COM-01:** A contract customer reported a complete loss of heating at one site. A Priority 1 request was created at 6:40 p.m. The customer contact was not informed until 9:10 a.m. the next day. The account manager learned about the request from the customer. The contract contains a general communication clause but does not specify incident notifications.

## Approved reference notes

**GOV-01:** The service director approves nonstandard service commitments.

**POL-01:** Priority 1 means complete loss of service at a customer site.
